The Extrait Difference

In the world of commercial perfumery, water and alcohol are used to stretch margins, resulting in scents that vanish before lunch. era operates on a different standard entirely.

Every bottle we produce is strictly an Extrait de Parfum, boasting a perfume oil concentration upwards of 30%. This isn't just about strength; it is about depth, complexity, and an unapologetic, room-filling projection. This is what creates a true "beast mode" performance, ensuring your invisible signature lingers for 12+ hours.

02

Global Reserves

A fragrance is only as potent as the earth from which it grows. We bypass mass-market chemical brokers to source raw, premium extracts from the most prestigious global reserves.

Whether it is the dark, smoky depth of aged Agarwood (Oud), the night-blooming richness of pure Jasmine Sambac absolute, or the earthy tension of crushed vetiver roots, our ingredients are selected for their raw intensity. These pure oils are then masterfully blended and aged, allowing the molecules to bond and mature before they ever touch glass.

The Anatomy of a Trail

A true luxury fragrance is not static; it is a living, breathing entity that evolves on the skin over the course of a day. This is how an era unfolds.

The Top Notes
Minutes 0 to 30

The introduction. Highly volatile, sharp, and magnetic. These lighter molecules—sparkling citruses, crushed spices, and fresh petrichor—are designed to grab immediate attention and pull you into the scent's narrative.

The Heart Notes
Hours 1 to 4

The soul of the fragrance. As the top notes evaporate, the robust floral absolutes, rich resins, and dense woods take over. This is the core character of the perfume, creating the primary scent bubble that projects around you.

The Base Notes
Hours 5 to 12+

The anchor and the memory. Composed of the heaviest, most luxurious molecules like aged leather, royal oud, cashmere wood, and dark musks. These notes bond tightly to your skin and clothing, leaving that unforgettable, lingering scent trail long after you have departed.
