Scent Trails

Chronicles of beast-mode performance. Unfiltered experiences and invisible signatures left behind by the individuals who wear an era.

I sprayed this twice on my jacket before a flight. Three days later, the flight attendant on my return trip asked me what I was wearing as I boarded. The projection on this extrait is absolute madness.

Marcus T. Wearing Midnight

It opens sharp and earthy, exactly like the air right before a heavy storm breaks. I have over 50 niche fragrances in my collection, and nothing behaves quite like this on the skin. It evolves beautifully over 12 hours.

David L. Wearing Monsoon

Completely unapologetic. It fills the room the moment you walk in, but in a way that makes people lean closer instead of pulling away. The damask rose and dark cedar dry-down is incredibly intoxicating.

Elena R. Wearing Crimson

There is a warmth to this that feels like liquid gold. I put it on for an evening event at 7 PM, and I could still clearly smell the melted amber and tonka bean when I woke up the next morning. True beast mode.

Sarah K. Wearing Golden Hour

A masterpiece of blending. The oud does not overpower; it supports the saffron and roasted coffee perfectly. It smells wealthy. It smells like old money and new ambition wrapped into one.

Arjun P. Wearing The Silk Route
